# Every puzzle is solvable by deduction alone

Reaching the solution never requires guessing and backtracking. A player who is stuck is missing a
deduction, not a lucky choice.

**Enforced by** Nothing. Asserted only.

**If violated** The player hits a wall that isn't their fault and can't tell whether they're stuck or
the puzzle is. Uniqueness alone doesn't prevent this — a board can have exactly one solution and still
be reachable only by guessing.
