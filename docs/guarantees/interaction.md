# Interaction

What the interface has to convey for a player to reason at all. A display characteristic
belongs here when its absence changes what a player can *rely on*, not merely how things look
— appearance conventions are a standard, not a promise.

Promises about how *fast* the interface answers are [latency.md](latency.md); promises about
what it must never show are [offline.md](offline.md).

_No promises yet._

The motivating candidate: a provisional note is always visually distinct from a committed
entry. A player who can't tell what they've decided from what they're still considering can't
reason, and reasoning is the entire activity.
