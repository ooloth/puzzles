# Puzzles

What a player is offered over time: variety, supply, and whether labels on a puzzle mean
anything. Whether an individual puzzle is *valid* — one solution, reachable by deduction —
is [correctness.md](correctness.md). The line is one puzzle versus the set.

_No promises yet._ Everything here becomes real as soon as generation is on the table.

Likely candidates once it is: a player is never served the same puzzle twice; a difficulty
label means the same thing across puzzles; the catalogue never runs dry. The last two are
open — see [Is difficulty graded, and does a grade promise anything?](../questions/is-difficulty-graded-and-does-a-grade-promise-anything.md)
and [Does v1 ship generated or seeded puzzles?](../questions/does-v1-ship-generated-or-seeded-puzzles.md).
