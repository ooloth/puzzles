# Is difficulty graded, and does a grade promise anything?

**Why it matters** "Every puzzle is solvable by logic alone" is already a guarantee. Whether
an *Easy* is guaranteed to be easier than a *Hard* is a separate and much harder claim to
make good on — it needs a difficulty model, not just a solver.

**Gates** what the generator has to measure while generating.
