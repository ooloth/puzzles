# What is the acceptable running cost, and is it a ceiling or a preference?

**Why it matters** Currently stated as not wanting to lose much money on this, which is a
direction rather than a number. A ceiling changes which platforms qualify; a preference
doesn't. The two behave very differently under a traffic spike.
